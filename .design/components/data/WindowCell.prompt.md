A franchise's competitive window, shown as a position on the ladder.

```jsx
<WindowCell stage="Contending" />
```

`WINDOW_STAGES` is an **ordered** sequence — Rebuilding, Retooling, Competing, Contending, Dynasty — running from low strength and trajectory to high. Do not draw it as a quadrant or a scatter plot: that makes an ordered position look like a coordinate, and it was the diagram users could not read.

Below ~500px the labels stop fitting five across; stack or show the active stage with its neighbours.
