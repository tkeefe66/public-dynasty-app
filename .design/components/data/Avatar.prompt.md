A franchise identity mark — colour plus initial. Stands in until a real Sleeper avatar is available.

```jsx
<Avatar name="Okafor" color="var(--id-2)" />
```

Identity colour appears in exactly two places: this mark and the 5px edge on a franchise hero. Never on a figure, a bar or a chart series. The glyph is fixed dark ink because every `--id-*` token is a light hue.
