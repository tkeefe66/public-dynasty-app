A section heading with an optional mono count on the right.

```jsx
<SectionHead title="Standings" aside="All-time · 12 franchises" />
<SectionHead title="Trades" aside="31 trades" collapsible open={open} onToggle={() => setOpen(!open)} />
```

Mixed case at 800 — never uppercase; Archivo 900 caps are the Agate voice. A collapse must hide **every** body belonging to the section (a ledger has two: rows and cards) and should default **open** on a phone.
